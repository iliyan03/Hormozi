>"If you don't have courage - nothing else matters."
> Alex Hormozi

If Hormozi could transfer one trait to his future son, he says it would be courage. Intelligence and other virtues accomplish little if someone cannot act, risk losing, or stand for anything.

He defines courage provisionally as taking action with:

- A large, immediate cost.
- An uncertain benefit.
- A delayed payoff.

Starting a business or podcast may bring mockery and repeated failure now, with no guarantee of later success. The current path may be guaranteed not to produce the desired life, while the risky path merely offers a chance. Potential is therefore related to how much uncertainty someone can tolerate and for how long.

Hormozi would rather his son become a failure than a coward: losing is an early signal on the path to winning, while refusing to play guarantees that the desired outcome never arrives.
